# Pre-registration 24 (draft, conditional): minimum-help credit at 7B on a fresh benchmark

Draft, October 27, 2026. Launched only if Pre-registration 23's pilot selects MH (or AVH) for the three-seed test;
otherwise withdrawn (or run with the averaged-value control as the candidate, per Pre-registration 23's rule).
Cost and the final arm list are fixed, and the file reviewed by Codex, before launch.

## Why

Codex's assessment (October 27): a method result reaches the acceptance range only if it holds at a second scale, on
problems outside MATH, against its closest mechanism control, and is stable under a larger answer budget.

## Setup

Qwen2.5-Math-7B, `train_dist.py` (FSDP2, 4 A100 80 GB per run, fp32 weights with bf16 autocast), Pre-registration 20's
recipe (16 x 8 rollouts, 2,048 tokens, AdamW 1e-6, 150 steps, constant normalizer 128 x 2,048), MATH training problems
in the same order. The minimum-help credit of Pre-registration 23 (hints of 0, 32, 64 tokens of the human solution, 2
continuations per level, 4 problems with at most 1 success in 8, 4 failed prefixes each of up to 256 tokens,
c_t = 2 (1 - t/100)) requires porting `mh_credit` to `train_dist.py` (continuations generated by the bf16 copy; credit
on prefix tokens through the FSDP model), with a parity check against `train_grpo.py` on frozen rollouts at 1.5B.

## Arms (seeds 8, 9, 10)

- **MH7:** Dr. GRPO plus minimum-help credit.
- **AVH7:** the averaged-value control (Pre-registration 23).
- **B7:** Dr. GRPO (seeds 8, 9 from Pre-registration 20; seed 10 trained here).

## Evaluation (eval_vllm.py; 32 samples at T = 1)

- **Primary: the fresh benchmark** `data/omni_hard_512.json` (Omni-MATH numeric-answer problems of difficulty >= 4,
  512 in a fixed random order, frozen October 27 before any model was evaluated on it), at 2,048 tokens, steps 0, 25,
  50, 100, 150 (B7 also T = 1.15 and 1.3 at step 150).
- Secondary: Pre-registration 20's 511 MATH test problems and hard cohort.
- **Budget stability:** step-150 checkpoints of MH7 and B7 at 3,072 tokens (Qwen2.5-Math-7B's context is 4,096 tokens,
  so the budget cannot double; 3,072 leaves room for the prompt), with B7's 3,072-token trajectory (steps 25-150).

## Decision rules (Pre-registration 21's estimator and guards; Holm over rules 1-2)

1. **MH moves the curve at 7B on the fresh benchmark:** MH7 against B7's trajectory-and-temperature envelope, at least
   +2, 95% interval above 0, positive in all three seeds, Holm-significant.
2. **Beyond the averaged-value control:** MH7 against AVH7's trajectory, at least +1, 95% interval above 0,
   Holm-significant, signs agreeing across seeds.
3. **Budget stability (reported, required for a claim):** MH7's gain at 3,072 tokens at least 80% of its gain at 2,048
   (paired bootstrap), with B7's 3,072-token trajectory as the reference.
Reported: generation tokens per step (compute), MH7's endpoint pass@1 and greedy accuracy against B7, the secondary
sets, and pass@16 by base-count stratum on the fresh benchmark.

## Cost (estimate, to be confirmed by a 3-step timing run)

7 training runs (about 1.7 times Dr. GRPO's step time for MH7 and AVH7 during steps 0-99): about 60-80 A100-machine
hours for training, about 15 for evaluation; about $2,400-3,000. Approval by the user before launch.
