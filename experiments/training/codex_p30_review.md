8. **TailSFT’s actual filtering criterion is relative improvement in length-normalized loss.** For each exact target sequence, precompute initial-model mean target-token NLL:
   \[
   \ell_{i,0}=-\frac{\log\pi_0(y_i\mid x_i)}{|y_i|}.
   \]
   At each encounter, compute \(m_{i,t}=\ell_{i,t}-\ell_{i,0}\). Drop the prescribed fraction of sequences with the **smallest, most negative margins** in the selection batch. Recompute the mask each encounter; filtering is not permanent removal. Optimize summed NLL divided by the **retained target-token count**, masking prompts and including the terminating end-of-turn token.

   The paper’s reported math configuration ramps the dropped fraction linearly from **0 to 0.5**. Its selection batch is 16 examples per distributed forward pass, distinct from the accumulated optimizer batch. If you retain an eight-example selection batch, explicitly label that adaptation and specify rounding, ties, and schedule progress. Define whether “2M tokens” means presented or retained tokens; report both and the initial-scoring cost. “Already fits” alone could implement a different algorithm. *(TailSFT, arXiv 2608.25756, Algorithm 1 and Appendix D.1–D.1.2.)*
