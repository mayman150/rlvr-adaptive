(A) Matched-accuracy gain as a covariance. With per-problem success p_x and P_k = E_x[1-(1-p_x)^k], for two policies at equal pass@1 the exact gain is E_x[(1-p_ref,x)^k - (1-p_arm,x)^k]; to first order it is Cov_x(w_k(p_x), delta_x) with w_k(p) = k(1-p)^(k-1) and delta_x = p_arm,x - p_ref,x (mean zero). An intervention moves the curve only by shifting success toward hard problems relative to the reference at the same pass@1. Since 1-(1-p)^k is concave, P_k at fixed mean is Schur-concave in (p_x): the curve's height is a statement about the dispersion (majorization order) of per-problem success, not about within-problem answer diversity.
