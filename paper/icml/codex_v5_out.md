3. **The three most important changes**

   - **Center the submission on the matched-accuracy audit.** Put an enumerated intervention/settings/seeds/verdict table in the main paper, exposing positive residuals, inconclusive results, exclusions and failed manipulation gates.
   - **Strengthen sensitivity where the conclusion is weakest.** Densify early 7B reference checkpoints, foreground the less saturated hard cohort, and show budget/prompt robustness for public outliers. Bootstrap precision conditional on a few trained seeds cannot support universal invariance.
   - **Finish P30 as the decisive prospective experiment.** Freeze forecasts, retain the random-SFT control and three seeds, and report forecast failures prominently. A controlled demonstration of when the frontier moves would make the paper substantially stronger than another collection of negative pilots.
