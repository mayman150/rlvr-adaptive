# Literature scan, October 27, 2026 (three subagent reports; arXiv/neurips.cc abstracts; [H] = from HTML full text, check before citing)

## Mechanism ("why one curve")
- RLVR weight updates are near rank-1 and change ~linearly: AlphaRL 2510.00553 (rank-1 component recovers >99% of gains, 8 LLMs, 7 algorithms); RELEX 2605.21468 / NeurIPS 2026 OR:FGnWADI4yz (Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B; extrapolating the rank-1 direction 10-20x matches full RLVR).
- RL updates sparse, overlapping subnetworks across seeds/data/algorithms (2505.11711, NeurIPS 2025); "Path Not Taken" 2511.08567 (same regions across recipes); TinyLoRA 2602.04118 (13 parameters).
- Prompt-gradient kernel kappa(x,x') = <grad p(x), grad p(x')>; pass@k vs pass@1 conflict only through cross-prompt covariance (Prompt Interference 2602.21189, NeurIPS 2026); negative interference / winner-take-all (2510.02230).
- Entropy mechanism R = -a e^H + b (2505.22617); implicit curriculum relay (2602.14872, ICML 2026); "RL squeezes, SFT expands" (2509.21128, ICLR 2026); two-stage shrink-then-expand (2510.04028).
- Our own: two-seed mixtures give no pass@16 gain over one seed (-0.3 to +0.1) at 1.5B and 7B: seeds improve the same problems.

## Supporting / closest prior
- Yue et al. 2504.13837 (NeurIPS 2025 oral): six RLVR algorithms similar; base beats RL at large k; distillation expands.
- Budgeted-search law 2609.01274 (EMNLP 2026 Findings): N_base ~ alpha N_RL^beta -- closest prior to a one-curve law (novelty risk; must differentiate).
- Invisible Leash 2507.14843; "Heel of RLVR" (NeurIPS 2026 position): +-20% advantage noise has no measurable effect.
- No paper found testing evaluation-length truncation as a source of frontier gains (our Check 16/21 is new).

## Threats (claims of off-curve gains)
- BBG 2606.15455 (Qwen2.5-Math-7B, MATH): pass@256 96.6 vs 93.0 at lower pass@1 (75.6 vs 77.5) [H]; no matched pass@1.
- ReCo 2607.26862 (Qwen2.5-Math 1.5B/7B, MATH; eval 16k tokens): pass@64 +3 [H]; reward rises more slowly (likely slower on the curve).
- PBA 2607.20543 (Qwen2.5-7B, Omni-MATH, 3 seeds): pass@1 29.0 vs 25.1 and pass@256 73.0 vs 68.3; GRPO early-stopped at pass@1 25.0 reaches 70.2 [H] -- strongest controlled off-curve claim.
- ProRL 2505.24864 / BroRL 2510.01180: long horizons and very many rollouts expand coverage.
- SCOPE-RL 2510.08141 (NeurIPS 2026): entropy control, one seed, pass@1 at T=0.1 vs pass@k at T=1.
- CoT-pass@k 2506.14245; Cover@tau 2510.08325 (metric challenges); Qwen2.5-Math special case (spurious rewards 2506.10947, contamination 2507.10532).

## Classic-RL ideas mapped to our failures (subagent ranking)
1. Problem-space whitening of the update: d = sum_j alpha_j g_j with alpha = (G + lambda I)^{-1} 1, G the sketched Gram matrix of per-problem gradients (natural gradient in problem space); not found in RLVR. First diagnose the kernel's top-eigenvalue share.
2. Optimizer/parameterization swap (SGD, Pion 2605.19282, LoRA r=1/64); SGD updates <0.02% of parameters (2602.07729); Flat-RLVR SAM 2610.29281 claims diversity at equal accuracy.
3. Weight-space operations without training (base-final interpolation, checkpoint averaging, seed averaging; WiSE-FT 2504.10478; Elastic Reset 2312.07551).
4. Bootstrapped LoRA ensemble (our seed-mixture check says no complementarity: likely dead).
5. SAM at matched pass@1, with PSN-GRPO as the sampling-side control.
- Count/novelty bonuses, Go-Explore resets, self-imitation, curriculum sampling (PLR): predicted on the curve by our results.
