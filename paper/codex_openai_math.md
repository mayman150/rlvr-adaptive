For your question, the key distinction is already in [paper.tex, lines 64–67](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/paper/icml/paper.tex:64): under independent sampling,
\[
P_k=\mathbb E_x[1-(1-p_x)^k].
\]
Redistributing probability among reasoning approaches while preserving every problem’s success probability \(p_x\) leaves every \(P_k\) unchanged. Thus approach diversity, answer diversity, and repeated-sampling coverage are different quantities.

You could cite these as **selected summaries describing multiple proposed routes, explicit obstructions, and repeated audits**. You cannot estimate the model’s exploration rate, strategy entropy, branch probabilities, time allocation, or abandonment frequency from them. Compression and selection can change all those quantities; even labeled verbatim excerpts do not restore the missing trajectory.

A concrete measurement proposal for your own saved rollouts would be:

1. Assign per-problem approach classes using the first substantive reduction or solution architecture, with manual audits of the labels.
2. Measure distinct approach classes and the probability that two sampled responses share an approach, conditioning separately on correctness and sample count.
3. For responses containing multiple attempts, annotate transitions as new approach, revisit, repair, or check.
4. Compare these quantities at matched pass@1 and matched decoding budgets, then relate their changes to the registered pass@16 residual.

This exposes something P32 cannot directly measure: **different correct approaches all occupy one outcome class**, while its manipulation check measures distinct incorrect final-answer classes and their collisions. [P32, lines 15–17](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/PREREGISTRATION_32.md:15), [64–74](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/PREREGISTRATION_32.md:64). It does **not** support claims that:

- **The base-model frontier was exceeded.** There are no corresponding base success counts, matched-accuracy comparisons, or controlled coverage measurements.
- **721 manuscripts or 373 families constitute a success rate over 4,000 problems.** Selection, aggregation, companion papers, and dependencies prevent that calculation.

- **All results are correct, novel, or independently verified.** The README describes differing verification stages and calls the manuscripts raw research data. The history records three withdrawals caused by one error, repairs to 14 other manuscripts, and consequential updates to 13 more. [README 7–12](/tmp/oaimath/README.md:7); [history 28–46](/tmp/oaimath/history.md:28).
