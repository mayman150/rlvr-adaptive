Verdict: the one-line summary sounds obvious, but a pre-registered null across ~12 interventions plus public checkpoints under one protocol is a legitimate corrective (lineage: Yue et al., Invisible Leash, DARS, BOPTR); the contribution is precision and breadth of controlled equivalence, not the concept. The SFT-start result is close to known (OctoThinker, Quagmires, TailSFT, LUFFY/ReLIFT); "+1.92 at 25K" is interesting only as a persistence-through-RL measurement on the same instrument that returned nulls for everything else.
Weak points: (1) "RL algorithms do not change coverage" too categorical (P33: 6 equivalent, 18 inconclusive, 14 no overlap; hard cohort zero equivalence; reference construction flips signs). (2) The instrument is blunt where rivals claim effects (±2 at pass@16 near ceiling cannot see pass@256/hard-set effects). (3) SFT-start effect confounded with finishing/format (starts truncate 45-47%; the earlier warm start shrank +4.77 -> +2.15 when the budget doubled; matched window steps 1-50 is truncation-heavy for B1k). The 2,048-token check is mandatory, plus boxed/truncation at eligible checkpoints and matched gaps at pass@64; a 25K SFT can contract per-problem spread (style homogenization) without touching new problems; the step-150 c=0 stratum is unmatched and on teacher-solvable-filtered problems. (4) Drop "targeting did not matter". (5) The Dong contradiction is wrong; OPD pass@64 matches SFT's; the self-SFT warm start retained +2.15 — say "starting distribution", not "teacher data". (6) The frozen law failed its forecast (+6.7 vs +1.9): it is descriptive of Dr. GRPO-from-base only.
H25 reporting: "Inconclusive under the pre-registered >= +2 rule; estimate +1.92, 95% CI [+0.68, +3.12], positive in all seeds, Holm p = 0.002: a nonzero positive gap is detected; its size is unresolved against the 2-point threshold (one-sided p that it exceeds 2 is 0.56)." Gap ~ start offset supports "RL preserves the offset" only through step 50; beyond that single-seed, descriptive (+1.5 -> +1.1 vs LH1, if anything shrinking). Do not write "persists through RL".
Framing: a measurement paper: "Under pre-registered matched-accuracy rules in a specified regime (Qwen2.5-Math 1.5B/7B, MATH, <= 2k tokens, <= 1k steps), no tested RL-algorithm intervention or public checkpoint departed upward from the base-set curve; a small-dose SFT start shifted it by ~1-2 points through early RL; the frozen law did not forecast the shift."
Demands: (1) format/finishing controls for H25 (format-only or self-generated-solution SFT at 25K, the 2,048-token check, matched gaps at pass@64 and per base-count stratum); (2) resolve the ±2 ambiguity and persistence (extend the reference's pass@1 support with more/longer B1k seeds, and/or a dose ladder); (3) report OPD's RL outcome, the law's failure, the Amendment-4 fallback and the full tallies at abstract level.
