**The bare finding is obvious. The controlled comparison could be publishable, but your proposed headline currently outruns the evidence.**

**1. Your novelty argument needs sharper boundaries.** Yue et al. LUFFY (2504.14945) and ReLIFT (2506.07527) exploit external demonstrations; OctoThinker (2506.20512) establishes initialization dependence; BOPTR (2609.01274) maps base search to RL operating points. “The start matters” is established. Your potential contribution is the precision and breadth of controlled equivalence, followed by a successful prospective prediction across starts.

**2. The controlled nulls are valuable; “RL algorithms do not change coverage” is too categorical.** [P31](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/results_p26/p31_analysis.txt:2) and [P32](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/RESULTS_32.md:66) establish checkpoint-average equivalence within ±2 points, conditional on trained seeds. Pass@2 diagnostics also depend substantially on reference construction. Moreover, your [draft](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/paper/icml/paper.tex:113) already reports a **self-SFT** warm start retaining +2.15 points [1.10, 3.20] at 1,024 tokens. Teacher SFT is therefore not uniquely established as the lever.

**3. Tiny dose is interesting, but does not identify the mechanism.** Learning from 48–56 demonstrations can transfer reusable mathematics, formatting, or stopping behavior. “Distributional shift rather than new knowledge” is not established by dose. Raw pass@64 improvement can accompany movement in pass@1; a start-conditioned forecast is not an observed RL curve. The reported +0.7–1.9 matched starting gaps are point estimates below your registered +2 threshold. With 45–47% truncation and changing boxed-answer rates, completion and extraction remain serious explanations. Your previous warm-start budget sensitivity makes this an evidenced concern, not hypothetical.

**4. “Targeting did not matter” is premature.** At 25K, [FORECAST_30](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/FORECAST_30.json) gives test pass@1 **47.55 for R versus 37.83 for H**. Their similar pass@64 does not establish equivalence of targeting effects. H also retains only **26/110 sampling-zero training problems**, after selecting teacher-solvable, short solutions ([Amendment 3](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/PREREGISTRATION_30.md:162)). This tests a filtered hard subset. Equal presented tokens also produce different numbers of examples and optimizer updates.

**5. The claimed contradiction of Dong is wrong.** Dong et al. (2609.28145) explicitly argue that similar pre-RL pass@k can conceal different **post-RL accuracy**. Your pre-RL coverage comparison cannot refute that. Furthermore, your [OPD start](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/results/worth-p30-stage2-s0-20261029-182830/OPD25_test/eval/step0_T1.00_eval.json) reaches pass@64 **92.56**, comparable to SFT’s 91.98/92.76. Raw coverage did not change uniquely through SFT. [P34](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/PREREGISTRATION_34.md:55) tests one reverse-KL dose; nominal token matching does not establish comparable optimization or supervision.

**6. Stage 2 distinguishes narrower hypotheses.** Persistence would establish a durable initialization effect within observed accuracy support. Disappearance would show this RL recipe erases this initial advantage. Neither establishes that starts determine every curve or that every start returns to one curve. If overlap covers only early checkpoints, persistence through step 150 remains untested, as [Amendment 4](/Users/mmayman/Desktop/adaptive-rlvr/experiments/training/PREREGISTRATION_30.md:194) acknowledges.

My top three demands:

1. **Separate completion from reasoning:** teacher-SFT, self-SFT, and format-only controls; common and native templates; audited extraction; larger-budget evaluation including the registered 2,048-token check.
2. **Establish trajectories, not isolated points:** replicate starting-data/SFT seeds, obtain dense common accuracy support, and report joint uncertainty across pass@2/16/64 and deeper hard-set sampling. Make OPD optimization comparisons credible.
3. **Demonstrate non-obvious predictive value:** the frozen start-conditioned law must beat zero-shift, initial accuracy/coverage, NLL, and BOPTR comparators. Disclose the post-test amendments and dose fallback. Frame the result as an approximate frontier within a specified regime.
